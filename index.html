THE STORY OF APPLE

Technology with
a human touch.

Apple Inc. is an American technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. It designs hardware, software and digital services that work together as one ecosystem.

1976

Apple was founded on April 1, 1976, by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak and Ronald Wayne.

FIVE DECADES OF INNOVATION

A journey that
changed technology.

1976

Apple is founded

Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak and Ronald Wayne establish Apple Computer Company.

1977

Apple II

The Apple II helps bring personal computing into homes and schools.

1984

Macintosh

The Macintosh introduces a new personal computing experience with a graphical interface.

2001

iPod

Apple introduces a portable music player that changes how people carry music.

2007

iPhone

A phone, an iPod and an internet communicator come together in one device.

2010

iPad

A new kind of multi-touch device arrives.

2015

Apple Watch

Apple brings personal technology and health features to the wrist.

2024
